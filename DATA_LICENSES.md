# Data and asset notices

The repository's MIT license applies to original code. It does not relicense publisher articles, transcripts, election files, fonts or map assets.

Every source has an attribution, rights basis and separate storage/model-use/redistribution status in `data/registry/sources.json`. Full acquired pages/PDFs remain in ignored local raw storage. Published factual observations and original factual paraphrases retain source citations and acquisition hashes. An allowed source record applies to those declared extracts, not the entire source publication. Source rights may change; contributors must retain the basis and scope of their review.

Unknown rights means metadata-only publication. Engagious recordings and transcripts are not redistributed based on ambiguous reuse language. Vote-Scope data is not integrated. Candidate contact details found in official files are discarded; only public electoral facts are exported.

State geography is Census-derived us-atlas data; see `apps/web/public/geo/LICENSE-us-atlas.txt` and its adjacent attribution. Archivo Black and Public Sans are distributed through Fontsource under their included SIL Open Font License notices in `apps/web/public/licenses/`. Source-specific licenses take precedence for these assets.
