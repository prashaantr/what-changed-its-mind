# Playful interactive essay

Audience: people arriving from X who enjoy visual experiments, plus developers and researchers inspecting/reusing the evidence. The product lets them understand and challenge what changes Jev's predictions. Voice: curious, playful, candid. Personal first-person framing expresses the user's actual project motivation without invented biography or outcomes.

Visual reference: https://www.us-elections.dev/en/how-it-works ; original implementation, identity and copy. Big editorial statements, numbered chapters, text next to interactive figures. Persistent map; accessible exact-data tables; receipt panel rather than modal overload. Avoid a generic dashboard card grid.

Archivo Black headlines + Public Sans body/labels, self-hosted with licenses. Blue #008FD5 and coral #FC4F30 for party data; gold #E5AE38 interaction; olive #6D904F, purple #810F7C auxiliary; paper #F0F0F0, ink #242424. Adjust text colors for contrast. Use numbers, labels, and patterns alongside color.

Nav Explore / Experiment / Sources / Contribute. Six chapters: meet battlegrounds; basics/polls; conversations; same facts/different order; receipts; did it help? The shuffle reveals saved order variants only, never simulated real results. Empty states explain why data is absent. Senate state boundaries; House district precision only after official geography verification. State-level locations must be labelled.

Mobile: compact graphic and explicit chapter buttons, no scroll trap. Reduced-motion removes animated transitions, not data. Charts have keyboard-accessible controls and equivalent tables. Personally identifiable author links remain omitted until supplied; contributors can configure a byline. Production never loads synthetic demonstrations as a forecast.
